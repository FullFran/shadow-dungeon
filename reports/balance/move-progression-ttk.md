Default = the starter's default loadout without Mejoras; Default ++ = the same moves at rank +2; Full ++ = its full-investment build (`FULL_BUILD`, Maestro moves where they pay) at rank +2. Speed-up = 1 − (TTK / default TTK), median over the fights both builds won (paired). The bots play the fast slot most and use boost and area on a schedule: they do not exploit zones, marks, i-frames or super-armor on purpose, so this is a floor for what a player gets.
