# Music shortlist — Mazmorra Sombría / Shadow Dungeon

Research date: 2026-10-25. Every license below was read on the file's own page
(OpenGameArt file page, artist page, or the artist's site-wide license page),
not on a collection page or a search snippet. Nothing was downloaded or
listened to, so "mood" comes from the author's description and tags. Audition
every pick before wiring it in.

## How to read the risk column

- **License** is quoted as the page shows it.
- **Content ID risk** means "could an automated fingerprint system flag a
  stream VOD or a YouTube upload of the stream". A valid license does not stop
  a claim; it only lets you dispute it.
  - **Low**: hobbyist OpenGameArt upload with no sign of commercial or
    streaming-service distribution on the page. Not proof of absence.
  - **Medium**: track is commercially distributed (ISRC, iTunes/Spotify), so
    it may sit in a fingerprint database, or third parties are known to file
    false claims on it.
  - **High**: the artist actively registers the catalogue in YouTube Content ID.
- Twitch VOD muting is driven by a third-party fingerprint service, not by
  YouTube Content ID. I could not load Twitch's current music guidelines page
  (it returned 404 after a redirect), so Twitch-side behavior is listed under
  "Unverified".

Superseded on 2026-10-25 for slots 3, 4, 5 and 8 by section 9 (PMD-feel
swap); *Boss Battle #2* is kept for a future boss.

   Pablo, CC BY 3.0, looped version included).

   Harder alternative: *Volatile Reaction* (MacLeod).
6. **Necrozma / eclipse**: *Final Battle of the Dark Wizards* (MacLeod,
   CC BY 4.0) for phase 1 or a cutscene, *Epic Boss Battle* (Juhani Junkala,

## 9. PMD-feel swap (2026-10-25)

User feedback: keep *Lonely Village* (title); make the rest feel closer to the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon soundtrack style (melodic dungeon themes, clear lead
over a steady groove, GBA/DS or SNES JRPG instrumentation, loopable, not pure
ambience), and later also replace the Aegislash track with a more PMD-like
boss theme while keeping *Boss Battle #2* for a future boss. No remixes,
covers or "inspired by <track>" uploads of Nintendo/Chunsoft music (none of
the pages below mention Pokémon or a remix; style tags such as "snes", "final
fantasy" or "Chrono Trigger" describe instrumentation, not a source melody).
Scott Buckley, Pixabay and anything with extra EULA terms excluded.

## Rejected and why

- **Pixabay Music** (all tracks): Pixabay Content License, not CC. Its own
  summary forbids standalone distribution, and a browser game serves the audio
  as directly downloadable files. Pixabay composers can also register tracks in
  Content ID, and there is no list of which ones are registered.
- **Scott Buckley as a default**: CC BY 4.0 is fine, but the artist runs
  "Smart Content ID" on YouTube, which places a claim on any video whose
  description lacks the credit (https://scottbuckley.com.au/library/copyright-claims-release).
  Kept only as fallbacks; if used, every YouTube re-upload of a stream needs
  the credit in its description.
- **Alexander Nakarada**: the old serpentsoundstudios.com address now serves an
  unrelated blog; his current site (creatorchords.com) says "Attribution
  required!" but I did not reach the full license text, and the CC BY 4.0
  claim rests on third-party pages. Rejected until the license text is read.
- ~~**Determined Pursuit (epic orchestra loop)**: listed in a CC0 collection but
  the page shows a resultant license of GPL 3.0 (per search; not opened).~~
  Corrected 2026-10-25 (section 9): the file page itself shows only "CC0" and
  the notice "This track is in the public domain as of January 2017"; it now
  plays on the collapse floor.
- **Town Theme** (CC-BY-SA 3.0 and GPL) and **Lose Music** (GPL 2.0/3.0,
  CC-BY-SA 3.0): share-alike/GPL, excluded by the brief.
- **Yet more game over fanfares**: CC-BY-SA 4.0 and chiptune.
- **Ancient Power Of Serpents** (OGA mirror of a Kevin MacLeod public-domain
  track): CC0 on the page, but the uploader states "I DID NOT MAKE THIS!!" and
  its original source, FreePD, has been permanently closed, so the provenance
  can no longer be checked.
- **Battle Theme A** (cynicmusic): now CC0, but early comments show it was
  CC-BY-SA 3.0 before a license change. Usable, but not picked to avoid the
  ambiguity.
- **Paid orchestral jingle packs on itch.io** (L.A.A. Orchestral Victory, 100
  win/lose fanfares): not free, licenses not shown.
