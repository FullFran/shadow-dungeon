## Visual effects

The attack and world effects are generated in the browser by [pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx) by Patch (patch.gg), MIT License, with its code in `src/vendor/pixelfx` and its licence text in `public/licenses/pixelfx.txt`. pixelfx adapts [Sprite Fusion's pixel art VFX](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx) by Hugo Duprez and [Hollow Crown](https://github.com/rehan-remade/hollow-crown) by rehan-remade, both MIT License (see `src/vendor/pixelfx/NOTICE.md`). Swords Dance, Will-O-Wisp, Phantom Force and the draining motes are made for this game in pixelfx's format.

## Tiles

The town and overworld tiles are crops of the [Ninja Adventure Asset Pack](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack)
by Pixel-Boy and AAA, under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (no attribution
required; credited anyway). Sources, crop rectangles and file hashes are in `public/tiles/town/LICENSE.txt`.
Water, bridges, chasms and rock in the overworld are drawn by the game's own code.

## Data

Which moves each line can learn is picked from [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co) (code under BSD 3-Clause). The recommended sets follow Smogon's competitive sets as exported by [data.pkmn.cc](https://data.pkmn.cc) ([pkmn/smogon](https://github.com/pkmn/smogon), MIT License). Only derived tables are kept in `src/data/learnsets.ts`.
